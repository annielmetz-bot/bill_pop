The problem

Great clinical tools. No way to bill insurance.

Clinics offering psilocybin-assisted therapy run on Homecoming or Althea. Both are strong at scheduling, intake, and cash payments — but neither can bill insurance, which is its own specialized, paperwork-heavy world.

So today that revenue is left on the table or chased by hand. Bill Pop is the missing piece: the engine that sits behind the clinic's existing system and does the insurance work for them.

From visit to paid claim

One assembly line, seven stations.

A patient visit goes in one end; a paid claim comes out the other. Two stations are built and tested today — the rest arrive month by month toward a December go-live.

Why it's built this way

Decisions that protect the clinic.

Rules as data

Built for rules that keep changing.

New Mexico's Medicaid board is still finalizing the codes and will keep revising them through 2027. Bill Pop treats those rules like editable settings, not logic buried in software — so an update is a data change, not a rebuild.

Swappable parts

Nothing undecided is hard-wired.

The clinic's source system and the clearinghouse are plug-ins. When a vendor opens direct access, or a clearinghouse is chosen, only that one connector changes — the billing logic behind it stays put.

Flag, don't guess

Raise a hand before mis-billing.

A record missing what it needs to bill is flagged for review, never quietly coded wrong. Under-billing loses money; mis-billing invites compliance trouble. The instinct is always to ask.

Privacy first

Audit trail on every touch.

Patient data is sensitive health information. Everything is logged, nothing sensitive sits in the open, and a clinic isn't switched on until the privacy agreement is signed.

Questions

Common questions.

Do we have to switch off Homecoming or Althea?

No. Bill Pop sits behind them — you keep your current system, and we handle only the insurance billing it can't do.

What if you can't connect to our platform directly?

We start with manual entry or a spreadsheet export, so nothing depends on a vendor integration. If direct access opens up later, only that one connector changes.

Which insurers do you work with?

We reach payers through a clearinghouse, starting with New Mexico Medicaid for the psilocybin program. The system is built to add payers as coverage expands.

Is our patients' data safe?

Yes. It's treated as protected health information — encrypted, audit-logged on every touch, and covered by a signed BAA before anything goes live.

What does it cost?

We're finalizing pilot terms with our first clinics. Reach out and we'll walk you through what makes sense for your practice.

When can we start?

The pilot goes live alongside New Mexico's soft launch in December 2026. Early pilot clinics shape what gets built between now and then.
